<div class="portfolio-prose" markdown="1">
I believe that language learning should be meaningful, communicative, and connected to students' real lives. My teaching focuses on creating a supportive and student-centered classroom where students feel comfortable practicing English, making mistakes, and sharing their ideas. I use authentic materials, role-plays, and interactive activities to help students apply what they learn to real-life situations. I also recognize that every student has different needs and learning goals, so I adjust my teaching based on their progress and feedback. My goal is to help students become more confident, independent, and motivated English learners.
</div>
