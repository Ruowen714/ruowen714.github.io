Beyond teaching, I have participated in various academic projects and campus activities at Wenzhou-Kean University. My interactive campus tour is available in the [Project section]({{ '/' | relative_url }}#section-project). I also served as a student advisor and humanities assistant, supporting academic events, translation, and student communication. As a student representative on a faculty search committee, I had the opportunity to evaluate candidates' teaching approaches and provide feedback from a student's perspective.

I have also gained experience in cross-cultural communication and student leadership. I worked as a Chinese-English simultaneous interpreter at an international sustainability forum and participated in the World Model United Nations Conference, where I represented Indonesia in discussions on global climate issues. As a member of my university's English Speech Team, I participated in public speaking competitions and mentored junior members. These experiences strengthened my communication and leadership skills and helped me work more effectively with people from diverse backgrounds.

Community service has also been an important part of my personal and professional development. I helped organize animal rescue and adoption activities and volunteered in community public-health programs, where I supported local residents through communication, coordination, and home visits. These experiences helped me develop a stronger sense of responsibility, patience, and empathy, which I believe are equally important in my work as an educator.
