Ruowen Sun

University of Pennsylvania · M.S.Ed. Teaching English to Speakers of Other Languages (TESOL)

I am an English educator with experience in adult ESL, K–12 literacy instruction, peer tutoring, and curriculum design. I am currently pursuing an M.S.Ed. in TESOL at the University of Pennsylvania. I also hold a B.A. in English from Wenzhou-Kean University and have studied at Columbia University as a visiting student.

My teaching focuses on helping students use English in real-life situations. At the Free Library of Philadelphia, I teach adult immigrants from different language backgrounds and proficiency levels. I design lessons around everyday topics, such as shopping, ordering food, visiting a doctor, and using public transportation. Through role-plays, group discussions, and authentic materials, I encourage students to practice English in situations they may encounter outside the classroom. I also have experience supporting young learners with reading and literacy skills through individualized instruction.

I believe that students learn best when they have opportunities to communicate, make mistakes, and receive meaningful feedback. Since students have different learning needs and goals, I try to adjust my teaching based on their participation, performance, and feedback. As an educator, my goal is not only to help students improve their English skills, but also to help them feel more confident using English in their daily lives.

My approach

Teaching Philosophy

A learner-centered approach to meaningful, communicative English teaching.

I believe that language learning should be meaningful, communicative, and connected to students' real lives. My teaching focuses on creating a supportive and learner-centered classroom where students feel comfortable practicing English, making mistakes, and sharing their ideas. I use authentic materials, role-plays, and interactive activities to help students apply what they learn to real-life situations. I also recognize that every student has different needs and learning goals, so I adjust my teaching based on their progress and feedback. My goal is to help students become more confident, independent, and motivated English learners.

Research

Project advisor: Leah Li V. Echiverri Project period: September 2023–March 2025

Does Communicative Language Teaching Approach Influence English Second Language Proficiency? A Case Study at a Sino-US University in Wenzhou (opens the official record in a new tab)

Leah Li Echiverri, Ke Wu, Ruowen Sun, Xinyue Zhao

Conference research and pre-recorded virtual presentation; an abstract is available, but no full paper was published.
The Asian Conference on Education & International Development (ACEID2025) · Abstract record published December 2, 2024

This descriptive correlational study examined how six Communicative Language Teaching approaches relate to the speaking and listening proficiency of English learners at Wenzhou-Kean University. It focused on role-play, opinion sharing, language exchange, information-gap activities, group work, and scavenger hunts.

My contributions

  • Wrote the literature review.
  • Designed questionnaires and interviewed 10 students.
  • Conducted data analysis.
  • Explored the classroom applications and advantages of information-gap and role-play approaches.
